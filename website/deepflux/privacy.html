Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Applies to the DeepFlux Android app and the DeepFlux desktop apps

The short version

DeepFlux collects no personal data, shows no ads, and includes no trackers or analytics. Everything you do in the app happens on your device. There are no accounts and no sign-ups.

What the app stores

  • Your settings, your download list, and your files — on your device only, in the app's private storage.
  • If you choose to save your own AI provider API key in Settings, it is stored on your device and is sent only to the AI provider you configured (for example DeepSeek or OpenRouter) when you send a chat message. It is never sent anywhere else.

Network connections the app makes

  • BitTorrent transfers: like every BitTorrent client, DeepFlux connects directly to other peers in the swarms you download from or seed to. Those peers can see your IP address, as is inherent to the protocol. This is not data collection — no central server receives it from us.
  • Downloads and metadata: the app fetches the files and pages you ask it to, from the servers those files live on.
  • Optional AI chat: if you use the AI assistant, your message is sent to the AI provider configured above to generate a reply.

What we never do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting in the Android app.
  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no tracking of any kind.
  • No selling, sharing, or profiling — we have nothing to share.

The desktop apps’ anonymous usage ping

The Windows and Linux desktop apps send one small anonymous heartbeat every few minutes while the app is open, so the website can show a rough “users online” count. It contains only a random per-install id, the app version, and the operating system — never your name, IP-linked history, downloads, or settings. You can turn it off in the app’s Download settings.

Permissions

The Android app requests only: network access (to download), network state (for the Wi-Fi-only guard), and notifications (to show download progress).

Children

DeepFlux is a general-purpose utility intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Your responsibility

DeepFlux is a download tool. What you download and share is your responsibility — respect copyright and the law in your region.
